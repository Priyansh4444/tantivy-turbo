Design two whole-shape alternatives for one next isolated experiment. Both
preserve source13 trusted metadata owner/coverage policy and unchanged literal
replay/publication/monotone callback/index bytes. Do not combine mechanisms,
introduce corpus IDs, alter metrics, or tune multiple numeric thresholds.

A: partition-preserving proposal. Compute expanded certificates into cold
temporary storage before publishing any final clause state. Admit expansion
only when final first_essential equals the original tight first_essential.
Otherwise execute the original tight certificate/partition byte-equivalent.
Prove rollback storage/ownership and original virtual-zero restoration; do not
re-seek live cursors or invent a score cache to undo a speculative certificate.
Tighter/wider optional bounds can still cost more even without promotion, so
this is no promised per-query performance floor.

B: bounded physical proposal. Cap source13's one-shot old-essential endpoint
at min(old-essential end, lo+128 physical documents), using the existing128
block size and checked/saturating address arithmetic. Keep full final coverage
and repartition, no promotion gate or additional provenance enum. Ground when
this actually widens and limits clone scan/candidate work; do not promise no
regressions merely from a bound on span. This is an alternative whole policy,
not a graft into A or a tuned family of widths.

Each package: caller usage first, exact local state/types/signatures/pseudocode,
module scope, coverage/strict-score/cursor/batch/tail/EOF/overflow proof, actual
fixture geometry and independent observers, source-grounded costs/limits,
alternatives rejected and rationale. Use real source and held receipts. No
compiler/test/worker/profile/browser/compression/commit/push or child task.
Write separately assigned durable paths and hold source worktrees unchanged.

Root selection rubric (0..5 each): (1) exact certificate/cursor ownership and
replay preservation; (2) existing score/provenance/API/format unchanged;
(3) smallest auditable state and no pass-through abstraction; (4) directly
addresses observed regression mechanism while preserving plausible region
reduction, with honest unmeasured limits; (5) realizable independent fixtures
for both chosen and rejected proposals, not implementation-mirroring tests.

No speed claim or universal Lucene API-parity result follows from this design.
